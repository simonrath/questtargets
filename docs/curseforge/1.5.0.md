# Quest Targets 1.5.0

- One download now works across Classic Era, Burning Crusade Classic, Mists of Pandaria Classic, Forever, and Retail. Choose `QuestTargets-1.5.0.zip` regardless of your client.
- Quest Targets automatically uses the appropriate quest data for your client.
- QuestieDB remains a separate addon for Classic Era, TBC, Mists, and Forever. Keep the matching QuestieDB addon installed and enabled.
- Retail continues to work without QuestieDB.

Close WoW and replace only the QuestTargets folder when updating.

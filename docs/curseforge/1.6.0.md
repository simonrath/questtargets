# Quest Targets 1.6.0

- **New button bar.** Open **Options → AddOns → Quest Targets → Buttons** to choose which targeting buttons appear side by side. Resize them together or right-drag any button to move the whole bar out of combat.
- **Three buttons, three roles.** The red Master button is your usual quest target button. The blue RestedXP button looks for targets from your current guide step. The yellow ItemTree button looks for mobs on your imported farming list.
- **Farm ingredients for recipes with ItemTree.** Open an ingredient’s **Sources** tab in ItemTree and click **Import mobs**. Use the yellow button or assign its hotkey in Quest Targets. The search prioritizes mobs in your zone and uses spawn distances where available. If a click finds nothing, press again to check more names. Expand **Show more** before importing to include those entries.

The same download supports Classic Era, Burning Crusade Classic, Mists of Pandaria Classic, Forever and Retail. Install ItemTree or RestedXP separately to use their buttons on a supported client. QuestieDB is a separate download where needed.

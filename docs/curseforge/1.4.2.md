# Quest Targets 1.4.2

- QuestieDB is now installed and updated separately and is no longer included in Quest Targets downloads.
- On Classic Era, Burning Crusade Classic, Mists of Pandaria Classic, and Forever, install and enable the matching QuestieDB addon from CurseForge before playing. Keep QuestieDB installed when updating Quest Targets.
- Retail continues to work without QuestieDB.

Available for Classic Era, Burning Crusade Classic, Mists of Pandaria Classic, Forever and Retail.
